# 🛒 Online Shopping Behaviour Analysis Dashboard

## 📖 Project Description

The **Online Shopping Behaviour Analysis Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, product performance, sales trends, and customer demographics. The dashboard provides interactive visualizations that help businesses understand customer behavior and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze online shopping trends and customer behavior.
- Identify top-selling and low-performing product categories.
- Understand customer demographics and purchasing habits.
- Monitor monthly sales trends and order distribution.
- Evaluate preferred payment methods and shopping devices.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Overview Dashboard

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Sales by Product Category
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Payment Method Analysis

### 2. Customer Analysis

- Sales by Gender
- Age Group Distribution
- Customer Segmentation
- Orders by Device Type

### 3. Product Analysis

- Top Products by Sales
- Sales by Brand
- Category-wise Orders
- Low Performing Categories

### 4. Time & Trend Analysis

- Sales by Location
- Monthly Sales Analysis
- Sales by Hour of the Day

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Clothing and Home categories generated the highest sales.
- Beauty category recorded the lowest sales.
- Most customers belong to the High-Value segment.
- Mobile devices are the most preferred shopping platform.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most used payment method.
- Monthly sales remain relatively stable throughout the year.
